Routine for the Kitchen agent (its Playground > "Create routine for this agent")

Name:
daily-stock-check

Agent:
kitchen

Prompt:
Check today's kitchen tasks and stock levels. Notify the supplier for any ingredient below its reorder level.

Trigger:
Recurring schedule, daily, at 9:00 AM (the Foundry portal's own default -- a
reasonable time for a bakery kitchen to check in).

To see it run without waiting for the schedule, open the routine and click
"Test run". Its run history shows every dispatch (manual and scheduled), with
how long it took and whether it completed.
